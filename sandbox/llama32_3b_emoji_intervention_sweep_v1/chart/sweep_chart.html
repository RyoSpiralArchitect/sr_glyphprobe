Which emoji actually pushes Llama-3.2-3B the hardest?

Each glyph measured on its own. Horizontal: how much simply prepending it shifts the next-token distribution. Vertical: how hard its residual-stream direction pushes when re-injected at a matched strength (α = 0.5 of the target activation RMS), as a ratio to the median of 24 random directions of exactly the same size. Above the dashed line beats that median — see the caveat below for what that does and does not license.

Out of contract. Exploratory sandbox run on the real bf16 weights. Not the sealed v2 experiment, no causal or semantic claim (pre-causal-activation-screen). Tokenization is controlled only inside the matched stratum shown here.
38 glyphs, every one costing exactly 4 prefix tokens — so nothing in this ordering is a token-count artefact. Hover for detail.
The token ladder. Shaded band = full range of the 4-token matched stratum above (line = its median). Spearman(token count, prompt KL) over all 50 glyphs = +0.06.

How to read it. The two axes are different questions. Horizontal is the everyday one — put this emoji in front of a sentence and the model's next-token distribution moves this much. Vertical strips out size: every glyph's direction is renormalised to the same RMS before injection, so height means "this direction is special", not "this direction is big". Spearman between the two axes inside the matched stratum is +0.55.

The vertical axis is a ratio to the null median, and the null is right-skewed. The assumption-free check is how many of the 24 random draws reached a glyph's KL: at layer 16 that is 0 for every glyph on the open-ended target, 27/50 on planet, and 0/50 on paris — no glyph clears the null on all three targets at any layer. Read the vertical ordering as relative, not as 38 individually significant results. The horizontal axis does not use the null at all and is unaffected.

What it does not show. A pre-causal activation screen only. It does not establish emoji meaning, semantic families, tokenizer independence, or any mechanism. The random-direction null is a size control, not a semantic control: beating it means the direction is structured, not that the structure is "meaning".
