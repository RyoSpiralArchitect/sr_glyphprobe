# Pre-causal metric map

[Japanese / 日本語](METRICS.ja.md)

GlyphProbe keeps intervention size, output displacement, and fingerprint direction
separate. No single metric is a semantic or causal score.

## Tokenization and source alignment

`tokenization.jsonl` records each glyph's code points, UTF-8 bytes, raw token IDs,
and the exact token IDs/counts of every sealed wrapper. Readiness separately
checks raw glyph token-count parity and per-wrapper total token-count parity across
the primary panel. Equal counts do not prove equivalent tokenization, so full IDs
remain part of the evidence.

## Source representation

`source_item_metrics.jsonl` records per-glyph activation RMS, wrapper dispersion,
and direction-replicate alignment. `source_layer_metrics.jsonl` records pairwise
cosine geometry, effective rank, balanced color × shape decomposition, and
matched circle-minus-square parallelism.

Source seeds are repeated estimates from wrapper subsets. They are not independent
target observations and must not be counted as such in a significance claim.

## Intervention fidelity and scalar balance

Every condition stores requested strength, achieved perturbation RMS,
perturbation-to-target RMS ratio, clip scale, clip hit, actual activation delta,
intended-versus-actual cosine, and post-activation cosine. The explicit zero-vector
condition checks that the hook itself changes neither activation nor logits.

`scalar_balance_summary.jsonl` aggregates these values separately for emoji,
generic-emoji, and random directions. It also records output KL and logit-delta
magnitude dispersion. Equal internal RMS does not imply equal downstream
displacement; use iso-KL only when it was actually enabled and its tolerance was
met.

## Distribution displacement

Each next-token distribution comparison includes forward/reverse KL,
Jensen–Shannon divergence, total variation, Hellinger distance, entropy change,
logit-delta norms, ranking overlap, argmax movement, margins, and top positive and
negative token IDs. These describe displacement, not meaning.

## Fingerprints

The full logit delta is CountSketched with a sealed seed and normalized to unit
length. The fingerprint statistic therefore primarily compares direction. Scalar
displacement remains available in separate metrics.

`fingerprint_summary.jsonl` contains deterministic held-out halves, repeated
group-stratified half-splits, within-target label permutations, random-direction
separation, and output-space color × shape decomposition.

`cross_seed_fingerprint_summary.jsonl` compares the same glyph across independently
estimated source directions. Random labels are regenerated by seed and serve as a
cross-seed null. Report the distribution of cells and clustered target structure,
not only the largest cell or the median. A finite-permutation p-value is a
screening statistic; it is bounded by the permutation count and is not a
multiplicity-corrected global test.

## Local linearity and dose

`sign_flip_summary.jsonl` compares positive and negative interventions. Perfect
local odd symmetry gives fingerprint cosine near `-1` and antisymmetry near `1`.

`dose_response_summary.jsonl` records adjacent non-decrease fractions and
last-minus-first displacement for KL, total variation, and logit-delta RMS.
Monotonicity over the tested doses is local evidence only; it does not establish
linearity outside the grid.

## Backend parity and speed

The MLX validator compares exact token IDs; baseline and intervened logits and
`resid_post` activations; zero-hook deltas; intervention fidelity; argmax agreement;
and logit/activation-delta normalized RMSE, cosine, and RMS ratio. Delta magnitude
gates prevent a directionally aligned but incorrectly scaled intervention from
passing.

The speed gate alternates synchronized end-to-end `backend.forward` calls over a
sealed prompt-length × layer matrix. It includes tokenization, capture and
intervention, device evaluation, and NumPy transfer. It is a backend-selection
measurement on one machine, not a general throughput benchmark.

## Optional SAE map

When explicitly configured, baseline and intervened activations are encoded and
decoded by the mapped SAELens SAE. Feature activity, reconstruction error,
explained variance, top feature IDs, and top-feature Jaccard are recorded. An SAE
is never inferred from a layer number, and an unexecuted SAE arm is missing
evidence rather than a null result.
