earlier utility builder remain historical; they do not make workshop rules part
of this preprint's current release conditions.

The archive manifest classifies each file. For mixed or third-party content,
this scope guide and its retained notices take precedence over a broad file
category. The paper and new data grant is explicit; the existing repository code
license alone is not treated as a grant for all embedded historical content.

Private author-tryout answers, assignments, packets, and notes are excluded.
Their absence does not imply consent for a later release, IRB approval, or an
ethics exemption. No claim of such approval is made.

Publication status and license are separate: confirmation of these terms does
not mean the archive has already been deposited, a DOI has been registered, or
the private source repository has become public. The public record, once
created, should identify this as a revised preprint and preserve these scopes.
