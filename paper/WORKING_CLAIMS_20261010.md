The [aggregate record](../validation/contextual_emoji_working_revision_20261010/summary.json)
is an explicit projection from rechecked completed analyses. It excludes item
assignments, individual answers, notes, participant metadata, and private paths.
The author requested local paper integration; this is not authorization to
publish the record or the private response material. The existing private
analysis, sealed reading, journals, and interface remain unchanged.

All contextual analyses are developmental and descriptive. The same 24 scenarios
are reused; their six families and repeated conditions constrain independence.
No population p-value, new confirmatory endpoint, free generation, or hidden-state
intervention is added. The development study uses no CLDR target. Its results do
not connect the earlier attention route to ordinary output behavior.

The intended next decision is qualification of the measurement, followed by new
independent materials and evaluators if warranted. Sample expansion, another
model, or natural-state causal intervention remains a separate, unexecuted step.
